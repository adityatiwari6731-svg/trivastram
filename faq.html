FAQ

Everything you need to know about KRAAL apparel, sizing, and fulfillment.

All KRAAL t-shirts and hoodies are tailored with a signature boxy, dropped-shoulder oversized fit. If you like the intended relaxed streetwear silhouette, buy your true size. If you prefer a standard regular fit, choose one size down.
Our oversized tees are crafted from 100% combed cotton French terry, and our hoodies use ultra-soft brushed fleece. All garments are bio-washed and pre-shrunk so they maintain their exact shape and weight through hundreds of washes.
When you place an order on KRAAL, our automated backend bridge sends your order directly to the Qikink production floor. The piece is printed with crack-resistant high-density ink within 24-48 hours and shipped directly to your door with real-time AWB courier tracking.
Production takes 1-2 business days, followed by 3-5 business days for courier transit across India (via Bluedart, Delhivery, or Xpressbees). You can track live courier milestones anytime using our Track Order page.
We offer 7-day doorstep size exchanges. If your size isn't right, reach out via our contact page and we will arrange a reverse pickup for exchange.
