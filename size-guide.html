HOW TO MEASURE AT HOME

  • 1. Chest Width: Lay your favorite tee flat on a table. Measure straight across from armpit seam to armpit seam. Double this number for full chest circumference.
  • 2. Body Length: Measure from the highest point of the neck ribbing straight down to the bottom hemline.
  • 3. Shoulder Drop: Measure from one shoulder seam straight across the collar to the opposite shoulder seam.
  • 4. Sleeve Length: Measure from the top of the shoulder seam to the end of the sleeve cuff.
