OUR PHILOSOPHY

TRIVASTRAM IS NOT JUST CLOTHING.

Born from the restless nights, late-night sketches, and unfiltered expressions of the modern underground. We craft heavy-gauge, boxy silhouettes with custom artwork designed to endure.

Fast fashion made clothes disposable. We built TRIVASTRAM on the counter-principle: ultra-heavyweight combed terry cotton, bio-washed, preshrunk, and built to survive hundreds of wears. Every silhouette features our signature dropped shoulder and wider armholes, delivering the authentic drape of contemporary Japanese and European street culture.
